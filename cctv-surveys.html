Precision Diagnostics

Eliminate Guesswork with Optical Pipe & Roof Inspections

Underground drainage issues and high-level roof leaks share a common challenge: you cannot fix what you cannot see without extensive disruption. At Reline-Drains Ltd, we deploy self-levelling HD camera crawlers and precision drones to inspect inaccessible areas with millimetre clarity.

Whether you are an architect submitting a build-over agreement to United Utilities, a homebuyer validating property infrastructure, or an insurer requiring defect verification, our diagnostic surveys deliver indisputable proof and actionable repair solutions.

CCTV Drainage Surveys

  • Homebuyer Pre-Purchase Surveys: Protect yourself from £5,000+ inherited drainage liabilities.
  • Build-Over Inspections: Certified pre/post extension documentation for Building Control.
  • Root Ingress & Fracture Mapping: Meter-accurate defect location for trenchless patch relining.
  • Rodent Ingress Tracing: Discovering dry bypass lines and rat entry holes into cavity walls.

Drone Roof & Valley Surveys

  • Zero Scaffold Hire Costs: Save thousands on scaffolding erection and pavement permits.
  • 4K Ultra-HD Close-ups: Pinpoint cracked ridge tiles, failed lead flashing, and damaged chimney stacks.
  • Commercial Roof Gutter Tracing: Survey industrial warehouses and high-rise commercial gutters in minutes.
  • Storm Damage Insurance Reports: Instant timestamped photographic evidence for insurers.

How Our Inspection Process Works

1

Probe Insertion

Our specialised push-rod camera crawler enters the line via existing manholes or access points.

2

Live 360° Scan

The operator rotates the optical head to inspect joints, connections, falls, and pipe wall condition.

3

Depth Mapping

Radio-detection sondes pinpoint exact defect coordinates and depths underground.

4

Written Report

You receive a certified digital report, video link, and fixed-price quotation for any repairs.
