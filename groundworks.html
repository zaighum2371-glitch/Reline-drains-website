Civil Engineering

Complete Subterranean Groundworks & Chamber Engineering

While trenchless no-dig lining is our primary repair method, certain drainage requirements demand physical civil groundworks—such as installing new connections for domestic extensions, replacing severely back-fallen pipes, installing off-mains septic tanks, or repairing sunken manholes.

Led by David Kershaw with 25+ years in civil drainage, Reline-Drains Ltd provides expert excavation, laser-guided laser gradient trenching, and certified deep chamber entry. Every installation conforms strictly to UK Building Regulations Part H and Environmental Agency standards.

New Drainage & Extensions

  • Extension Connections: Installing new foul runs, inspection chambers, and rest bends.
  • Surface Water Attenuation: Soakaways, rain gardens, and modern cellular crate systems.
  • Sewer Re-alignment: Correcting reverse falls and dips (bellies) where water stagnates.
  • Neat Surface Reinstatement: Relaying block pavers, concrete, gravel, and lawns cleanly.

Septic Tanks & Confined Spaces

  • Septic Tank Upgrades: Environment Agency General Binding Rules compliant installations.
  • Treatment Plant Servicing: Mechanical aerator replacement, desludging, and discharge checks.
  • Deep Chamber Rebuilding: Bench-grouting, step iron installation, and heavy-duty D400 cover fitting.
  • Certified Confined Space Entry: Full gas detection, rescue harness, and breathing safety apparatus.

Frequently Asked Questions: Groundworks & Septic Tanks

Answers to common civil drainage, septic tank, and compliance queries.

New drainage runs, building extensions, and septic tanks must comply with UK Building Regulations Part H. We design, install, and pressure-test systems to meet local authority Building Control standards seamlessly.
Under Environment Agency General Binding Rules, septic tanks can no longer discharge directly into ditches or surface watercourses. They must connect to a compliant drainage field (soakaway) or be upgraded to a package sewage treatment plant.
Our engineers hold certified City & Guilds Confined Space Entry qualifications, utilising multi-gas detectors, rescue tripods, safety harnesses, and forced air ventilation for chambers deeper than 1.2 metres.
We install engineered land drainage solutions including perforated French drains, high-capacity attenuation crates, silt traps, and dedicated soakaway fields to divert runoff safely away from foundations.
