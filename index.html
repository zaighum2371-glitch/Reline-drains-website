Drainage Advice & Emergency Guide

What to Do When You Suspect a Drainage Emergency

Practical guidance from Dave Kershaw (25+ years experience) on spotting serious faults early and protecting your home.

5 Warning Signs of Drain Failure

  • Gurgling sounds from plug holes & toilet bowls.
  • Persistent foul sewer smells inside or outside.
  • Sinks, baths, or washing machines emptying sluggishly.
  • Soggy, sunken, or lush grass patches over pipe runs.
  • External manholes overflowing or backing up.

Who Pays: You or United Utilities?

Understanding drainage ownership saves time and money:

Private Drains (Homeowner): Pipes inside your boundary serving solely your property. We unblock and repair these rapidly.
Lateral Drains & Sewers (Water Co): Pipes shared with neighbours or beyond your boundary. Maintained by United Utilities.

Emergency Action Steps

If sewage is backing up into your bathroom or kitchen:

  1. Stop running taps and turn off dishwashers/washing machines.
  2. Avoid chemical unblockers which can emit toxic fumes and damage pipework.
  3. Keep children and pets away from standing grey water or manholes.
