What is Cured-In-Place Pipe (CIPP) Relining?

Trenchless pipe relining (also known as CIPP lining) is the industry standard for repairing subterranean pipe damage without excavation. Rather than digging trenches across expensive block paving, tarmac drives, or manicured gardens, we install a new structural sleeve directly inside the old damaged pipe.

A flexible woven textile liner impregnated with thermosetting epoxy or silicate resin is inverted into the damaged run using compressed air or water pressure. Once in place, the resin cures into a rock-solid, glass-smooth pipe that bonds structurally to the original host pipe, sealing cracks, stopping root ingress, and bridging misaligned joints permanently.

Our 4-Step Relining Process

1

High-Def CCTV Survey

Continuous camera footage maps exact defect locations, bends, and lateral branch junctions.

2

Mechanical Descaling

Rotary chains and hydro-cutters clear tree roots, scale, and silt to ensure maximum resin adhesion.

3

Liner Inversion

The custom-measured resin liner is inverted into the pipe run and inflated under pressure.

4

Curing & Sign-off

The resin cures solid; a final CCTV recording confirms a seamless, airtight structural repair.
