How High-Pressure Water Jetting Solves Your Blockage

Unlike standard plunger tools or mechanical rods that only punch a temporary puncture hole through a blockage, high-pressure water jetting (hydro-jetting) thoroughly flushes and scours the full 360-degree interior surface of your underground pipework.

Our onboard high-pressure pump units deliver powerful, controlled water streams at up to 4,000 PSI. This instantly breaks down compacted fatbergs, encrusted limescale, tree root mats, builder's silt, and wet wipe accumulations without damaging the host pipe.
